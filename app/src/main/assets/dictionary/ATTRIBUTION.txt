Transformations applied to this pilot subset:
- Filtered to 6 common HSK 1 words
- Numbered pinyin (e.g., "ma1 ma5") converted to tone-marked pinyin (e.g., "māma")
- Spaces removed between syllables to match app display convention
- Uppercase initials normalized to lowercase
- Definitions: "/" separators replaced with "; "
- HSK level taken from CC-CEDICT's own tagging
- No radical, stroke, example, or verification data added (left empty/unknown)
