Dictionary: 557 words from CC-CEDICT, cross-referenced with HSK 3.0
word list (ivankra/hsk30, MIT license) for HSK level tagging:
507 HSK 1 words and 50 HSK 2 words.

Transformations applied:
- Filtered to HSK 1 and HSK 2 words (557 entries, 557 matched with CC-CEDICT definitions)
- Numbered pinyin (e.g., "ma1 ma5") converted to tone-marked pinyin (e.g., "mama")
- Spaces removed between syllables to match app display convention
- Uppercase initials normalized to lowercase
- Definitions: "/" separators replaced with "; ", truncated to 60 chars per definition
- HSK level taken from HSK 3.0 word list (level 1 or 2)
- No radical, stroke, example, or verification data added (left empty/unknown)
